## Synthesis for the app audit

1. **Supported qualitatively:** premarked areas, repeated price interactions, entry observations on five-minute or one-hour views, broader higher-timeframe context, correlated Nasdaq inputs, volume around key areas, prior pivots as potential exits, and waiting when no setup appears.
2. **Not established by these five transcripts:** five agreeing leaders with one opposing allowed; another exact numerical quorum; a requirement that reactions all begin on the same candle; the app's fifteen-minute reaction persistence; the exact zone tolerance, event lifetime, stop placement, or target algorithm.
3. **Keep separate:** simultaneous current observations are an engineering comparison rule; simultaneous reaction origins are a different claim. These lessons do not provide an exact numerical rule for either.
4. **Context presentation:** show higher-timeframe structure and disagreement honestly. The sources give higher timeframes weight and also show bias changing after another timeframe is examined. They do not justify inventing a mechanical trend veto from transcript language alone.
5. **Promotion status:** the proposed five-agree/one-opposing setting remains an owner-selected app interpretation. This review does not validate it as the creator's formula, demonstrate profitability, or authorize deployment or live activation.
