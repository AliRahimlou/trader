### Practical interpretation

- **Broader context:** a transparent closed-candle structure display can describe higher-timeframe direction. Label it as the app's defined measure, show its evidence and age, and separate it from permission to enter. The creator's discretionary trend judgments are not a specification for a strict swing algorithm.
- **Participation across time:** already-established rejection and continued selling/buying can remain relevant. The source does not require all leaders to touch a zone at one instant. If automation persists evidence, declare the start, expiry, continued-direction requirement, and invalidation; a three-bar window is an engineering choice.
- **Conflict:** distinguish opposing evidence in different companies from a contradictory reading within one company, neutral/no setup, and missing/stale data. A lone dissenter is not automatically equivalent to broad disagreement, but these sources do not determine the acceptable dissent count.
- **Entry/exit:** preserve source-method identity, instrument, entry timeframe, location evidence, VIX timeframe, stop invalidation, and target. The five-minute scalp and hourly break/retest explanations should not be represented as one fully specified formula.
- **Evaluation:** none of these demonstrations supplies an audited return series, transaction-cost analysis, or unseen validation of this app. Freeze candidate rules before testing them; record every variant tried. No transcript wording establishes an edge or guarantees execution profits.
