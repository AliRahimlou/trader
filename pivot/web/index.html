    How execution works

    QQQ is the chosen Alpaca instrument. Mechanical choices fill in details absent from the clips: fixed areas confirmed by repeated touches or crossings, a 0.1% band, five-minute leader reactions, four of seven leaders agreeing with none opposing, a stop beyond the event/zone and a target at the next opposing level. The videos do not specify the colored-area formula; this app uses a documented approximation. Reactions last at most 15 minutes, and opportunities expire 180 minutes after the original event or at session change. Review the full rules when turning on live money.

    The saved dollar amount is the purchase target. Exits close the shares held, so proceeds vary with price. Only one position is managed at a time. New entries stop ten minutes before market close; the app starts closing positions five minutes before close.

    Current limits

    Actual VIX data is required for every entry. A missing or delayed feed keeps the app waiting even when Live money is on. Stocks currently use the labeled provider feed. Offline broker simulations cover order handling; paper-account and live fills have not been verified. Historical testing has not established profitability.

    Broker stops are submitted after fills. Fractional DAY stops expire at the session’s end. The host machine must remain running and connected for entry management, target exits and end-of-day exits. Buy stops for short positions may be converted to stop-limit orders by Alpaca.
